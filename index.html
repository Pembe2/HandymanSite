About North Beach Repair

Building a dependable local repair business with care and intention.

North Beach Repair is being developed as a part-time handyman business with the goal of growing into a full-time service for the NH Seacoast. My mission is simple. Respond to inquiries in a timely manner and show up when scheduled to provide careful, reliable home repair work while building a business that creates more flexibility and time for my family.

My background in mechanical engineering shaped the way I approach repair work: perform a detailed inspection, understand the problem, and choose a clean, durable fix. My focus is on straightforward communication, thoughtful workmanship, respect for the homes where the work is done, and doing the job properly without cutting corners or relying on short term solutions.

Services we provide

Focused help for common home repair needs

Practical repair, installation, and finish work for homeowners, rental properties, and small businesses across the NH Seacoast.

Drywall & Surface Repair

Patch holes, repair cracks, prep wall surfaces, and blend touch-up paint for a clean finished result.

Painting & Finish Work

Refresh rooms, trim, doors, closets, and rental turnovers with careful prep and neat paint lines.

Doors, Trim & Hardware

Adjust sticking doors, tighten hinges, repair loose trim, caulk gaps, and tune up everyday hardware.

Mounting & Installation

Install shelves, mirrors, hooks, curtain hardware, artwork, and wall-mounted fixtures with secure placement.

Flooring Touch-Ups

Handle small flooring repairs, plank replacements, transitions, thresholds, and minor finish corrections.

Punch-List Repairs

Group small repairs into one efficient visit for move-ins, move-outs, sale prep, or overdue home fixes.
