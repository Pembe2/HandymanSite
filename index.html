About North Beach Repair

Building a dependable local repair business with care and intention.

North Beach Repair is being developed as a part-time handyman business with the goal of growing into a full-time service for the NH Seacoast. My mission is simple. Respond to inquiries in a timely manner and show up when scheduled to provide careful, reliable home repair work while building a business that creates more flexibility and time for my family.

My background in mechanical engineering shaped the way I approach repair work: perform a detailed inspection, understand the problem, and choose a clean, durable fix. My focus is on straightforward communication, thoughtful workmanship, respect for the homes where the work is done, and doing the job properly without cutting corners or relying on short term solutions.
